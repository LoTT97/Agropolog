Our story

A kitchen rooted in the land

Agropolog started as a small family farm on the edge of the valley. Today we grow more than forty kinds of vegetables, keep our own bees, and bake bread every morning in a wood-fired oven.

Our menu changes with the seasons — what's ripe this week decides what's on your plate tonight.
